Engine Architecture

Deterministic Verification + Explainable Heuristics

Why we refuse black-box generative models for regulatory verification.

STAGE 1: INGESTION
Edge Privacy Sanitizer

Regex-based in-memory masking of PAN, Aadhaar, Phone, Bank, and UPI handles before any analytical processing.

STAGE 2: EXTRACTION
Claim & Entity Parser

Deterministic extraction of promised returns, timelines, claimed registration IDs (`INA...`), URLs, and payment requests.

STAGE 3: VERIFICATION
SEBI & Registry Matcher

Validates registration formats, authentic prefixes, authorized domain whitelists, and flags lookalikes via Levenshtein distance.

STAGE 4: SYNTHESIS
Epistemic Uncertainty Engine

Enforces calibrated separation: "Verified Facts" vs "What We Could Not Verify" + Grassroots Hindi analogy generator.

Execution Latency: < 20ms (Synchronous)
Automated Tests: 44 / 44 Passing (Vitest)
Client Memory Footprint: < 15 MB RAM
Guardrail Compliance (15% Weight)

Regulatory Trust by Design, Not by Accident

Strict adherence to SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 and DPDP Act, 2023.

🚫 Non-Advisory Mandate

The engine strictly refuses to provide stock tips, buy/sell recommendations, or price targets. Queries like "Which stock should I buy?" trigger an instant educational refusal explaining that only certified SEBI RIAs can advise.

🏛️ Absolute Commercial Neutrality

No broker referrals, no sponsored trading accounts, and no affiliate revenue models. Designed strictly as a neutral, trusted public utility for SEBI and NSDL.

⚖️ Epistemic Honesty (No Hallucination)

Never invents registry verification. If an entity or SEBI ID is unknown, it is unequivocally marked "Could Not Verify" with explicit disclosure of uncorroborated claims.

🛡️ DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

Zero persistence of user financial data. Zero telemetry logging of unredacted inputs. Zero transmission of citizen financial queries to offshore third-party LLM APIs.

Our Legal Line: “SANGYAN KAVACH does not tell you what to buy. It helps you understand what you are being told.”
